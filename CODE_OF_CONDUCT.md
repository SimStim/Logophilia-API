# Code of Conduct

It's simple: **Behave.**

Be respectful, be constructive, and treat others as you'd like to be treated.

- Disagreement is fine. Personal attacks are not.
- Criticism is welcome. Harassment, insults, threats, and discrimination are not.
- Keep discussions relevant and constructive.
- Don't spam, troll, or deliberately disrupt the project.

In short: You don't have to agree with everyone, like everyone, or even be particularly fond of everyone. Just behave
like a civilized human being.

The project maintainer reserves the right to remove inappropriate contributions, close discussions, or restrict
participation when necessary. No lengthy explanation or debate is required.

That's it. Common courtesy isn't rocket science.
